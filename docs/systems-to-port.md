# Air physics to switch (BBS / KH1 / KH2)

Examples, not the full list. Look in all three games for related air mechanics nobody mentioned,
say what and where, and ask before adding. Status is "not yet looked at" unless noted.

- **Jump:** take-off speed, how long holding jump keeps rising (if the game has a held jump),
  apex, jump height for each character.
- **Gravity:** how fast vertical speed drops each tick, rising and falling.
- **Fall-speed cap:** the fastest a character can fall.
- **Air steering and momentum:** how much the stick moves the character in the air, how ground
  speed carries into a jump, air turning.
- **Landing:** what carries over on landing (momentum, landing lag).
- **Falls off ledges:** walking off an edge versus jumping.
- **High Jump / jump-height abilities** (KH1 and KH2 growth levels) and how they map to BBS's
  own jump boosts. Open design choice: ask before deciding.
- **Things that stay BBS's own unless a tester asks:** Glide, Superglide, Doubleflight, air
  dodges (Air Slide, Sonic Impact etc.), air Command Styles, Shotlocks, D-Links, Thunder Surge
  and other commands that move the character in the air.

Rules for every item:
- With the setting on BBS, BBS's behaviour is untouched for all three characters.
- KH1 / KH2 values are exact, converted only through BBS's checked tick rate.
